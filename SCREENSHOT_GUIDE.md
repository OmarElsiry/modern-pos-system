# 🖼️ JOECASHIER Screenshot Guide

To sell JOECASHIER effectively, you need a high-impact visual portfolio. Follow this guide to capture the most persuasive UI regions.

## 📸 1. The "Transaction Engine" (POS Screen)
*   **Target**: The active cart and payment action.
*   **Elements to Include**:
    *   A cart with at least 5 different items.
    *   One item using the "Wholesale" price toggle to show flexibility.
    *   The "Customer Selection" dropdown showing a loyal customer's name.
    *   The "Total Amount" button glowing or in a hovered state.
*   **Why**: It proves the software is ready for real-world retail speed.

## 📸 2. The "Intelligence Hub" (Dashboard)
*   **Target**: The overview analytics.
*   **Elements to Include**:
    *   Line chart showing a steady "Revenue" climb (use sample data if needed).
    *   The "Low Stock" alert cards showing items in critical red.
    *   The "Quick Stats" bento-box showing Total Sales and Invoice Count.
*   **Why**: It appeals to the business owner's need for control and data-driven decisions.

## 📸 3. The "Orderly Warehouse" (Product Inventory)
*   **Target**: The product management table.
*   **Elements to Include**:
    *   Glassmorphic table styling.
    *   Clear category badges (e.g., "Beverages", "Snacks") in different colors.
    *   The search bar with a partial term showing live filtering.
*   **Why**: It demonstrates that managing thousands of items is effortless.

## 📸 4. The "Professional Face" (Ready-to-Print Invoice)
*   **Target**: The PDF/Thermal receipt preview.
*   **Elements to Include**:
    *   Your custom logo centered.
    *   Clean, high-contrast text in Arabic and English.
    *   The "Return Policy" or "Thank You" note at the footer.
*   **Why**: It's the only part of the app the merchant's customer sees; it builds professional trust.

## 📸 5. The "Dark Mode Luxury" (Settings)
*   **Target**: The settings screen.
*   **Elements to Include**:
    *   Toggle buttons and premium typography.
    *   The "Store Name" updated to the merchant's brand.
*   **Why**: Shows the "Premium" feel that separates it from cheap alternatives.

---
*Tip: For the best results, use a 1920x1080 resolution and ensure the browser zoom is set to 100%.*
