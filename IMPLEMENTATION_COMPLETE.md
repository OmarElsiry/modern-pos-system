## 🎓 User Guide

### Getting Started:

**1. Add Products:**
- Go to "المنتجات" (Products)
- Click "إضافة منتج جديد"
- Fill in details
- Set minimum stock level
- Save

**2. Add Customers (Optional):**
- Go to "العملاء" (Customers)
- Click "إضافة عميل جديد"
- Fill in details
- Save

**3. Make a Sale:**
- Go to "نقطة البيع" (POS)
- Select pricing type (wholesale/retail)
- Select customer (optional)
- Search or scan products
- Add to invoice
- Complete invoice

**4. View History:**
- Go to "الفواتير" (Invoices)
- Search or filter
- Click "التفاصيل" for details

**5. Check Stock:**
- Go to "المنتجات" (Products)
- Look for alert badge
- Click badge to see low stock items

## 💡 Tips & Best Practices

### For Best Results:
1. **Set realistic minimum stock levels** (not too high/low)
2. **Add customer phone numbers** (for easy search)
3. **Use quick search** (faster than barcode for known items)
4. **Check alerts daily** (prevent stockouts)
5. **Review invoice history weekly** (spot trends)

### Performance Tips:
1. **Keep database under 1 GB** (archive old data)
2. **Close unused screens** (save memory)
3. **Restart app weekly** (clear cache)
4. **Backup regularly** (use backup service)
