# 🎥 Video Tutorial Script - POS Cashier System

**Complete walkthrough for training staff and new users**

---

## 📹 Tutorial 1: Getting Started (5 minutes)

### Introduction (30 seconds)
"Welcome to the POS Cashier System! This is a complete point of sale solution that will help you manage your business efficiently. In this tutorial, we'll show you how to get started."

### First-Time Setup (2 minutes)

**Scene 1: Opening the App**
1. "First, let's open the application by double-clicking the icon"
2. "The app will open and show you the Dashboard"
3. "This is your business overview - you'll see sales, products, and alerts here"

**Scene 2: Configure Business Information**
1. "Click on the Settings icon (⚙️) in the menu"
2. "Here you can enter your business information"
3. "Fill in your store name - this will appear on receipts"
4. "Add your address and phone number"
5. "If you have a tax number, add it here"
6. "Click Save to store your information"
7. "You can see a live preview of how your receipt will look"

**Scene 3: Add Your First Category**
1. "Click on Categories (📁) in the menu"
2. "Click 'Add New Category'"
3. "Enter a category name like 'Electronics' or 'Food'"
4. "Click Save"
5. "Repeat for all your product categories"

**Scene 4: Add Your First Product**
1. "Click on Products (📦) in the menu"
2. "Click 'Add New Product'"
3. "Enter the product name"
4. "Add a barcode if you have one"
5. "Select the category"
6. "Enter wholesale and retail prices"
7. "Set the stock quantity"
8. "Set minimum stock level for alerts"
9. "Click Save"

### Conclusion (30 seconds)
"Great! You've completed the basic setup. Your system is now ready to make sales. In the next tutorial, we'll show you how to use the POS screen."

---

## 📹 Tutorial 2: Making Your First Sale (5 minutes)

### Introduction (20 seconds)
"Now that your system is set up, let's learn how to make a sale using the POS screen."

### Using the POS Screen (3 minutes)

**Scene 1: Navigate to POS**
1. "Click on Point of Sale (🛒) in the menu"
2. "This is your main checkout screen"

**Scene 2: Select Pricing Type**
1. "First, choose your pricing type"
2. "Select 'Wholesale' for bulk sales"
3. "Or 'Retail' for individual sales"
4. "The prices will adjust automatically"

**Scene 3: Add Products (3 Methods)**

**Method 1: Barcode Scanner**
1. "If you have a barcode scanner, just scan the product"
2. "The product will be added automatically"

**Method 2: Quick Search (Recommended)**
1. "Press F2 on your keyboard"
2. "A search box will appear"
3. "Type the product name or barcode"
4. "Click on the product from the dropdown"
5. "The product is added to your invoice"
6. "Press ESC to close the search"

**Method 3: Manual Entry**
1. "Type the barcode in the barcode field"
2. "Press Enter"
3. "The product is added"

**Scene 4: Adjust Quantities**
1. "Click on the quantity field"
2. "Change the number"
3. "Press Enter or click outside"

**Scene 5: Select Customer (Optional)**
1. "Click the customer dropdown"
2. "Search for the customer"
3. "Select them from the list"
4. "The sale will be linked to this customer"

**Scene 6: Complete the Sale**
1. "Review the items and total"
2. "Click 'Complete Invoice'"
3. "Confirm in the dialog"
4. "A receipt preview will appear automatically"

**Scene 7: Print Receipt**
1. "Click 'Print' to print the receipt"
2. "Or click 'Download' to save as PDF"
3. "Click 'Close' to continue selling"

### Conclusion (40 seconds)
"Congratulations! You've made your first sale. The invoice is saved automatically, and you can print receipts for your customers. Practice this a few times to get comfortable with the workflow."

### Introduction (20 seconds)
"Let's learn how to manage your inventory and keep track of stock levels."

1. "Click 'Edit' on a product"
2. "Update the quantity field"
3. "Click Save"
4. "The stock is updated immediately"

**Scene 4: Edit Product Details**
1. "You can also update prices"
2. "Change the category"
3. "Update the minimum stock level"
4. "All changes are saved to the database"

### Customer Management (1 minute)

**Scene 1: Add Customers**
1. "Click on Customers (👥) in the menu"
2. "Click 'Add New Customer'"
3. "Enter the customer name (required)"
4. "Add phone number (recommended)"
5. "Add email (optional)"
6. "Click Save"

**Scene 2: View Purchase History**
1. "Find a customer in the list"
2. "Click 'View Purchases'"
3. "See all their invoices"
4. "See total amount spent"

### Conclusion (40 seconds)
"Now you know how to manage your inventory and customers. Check stock alerts daily to prevent running out of popular items."

## 🎬 Production Notes

### Recording Tips:
1. **Screen Recording:** Use OBS Studio or similar
2. **Resolution:** 1920x1080 minimum
3. **Frame Rate:** 30fps minimum
4. **Audio:** Clear voice, no background noise
5. **Cursor:** Make cursor visible and large
6. **Pace:** Speak slowly and clearly
7. **Pauses:** Pause after each action
8. **Highlights:** Use cursor highlights for clicks

### Editing Tips:
1. Add text overlays for key points
2. Zoom in on important areas
3. Add arrows to highlight buttons
4. Include keyboard shortcut overlays
5. Add chapter markers
6. Include intro and outro
7. Add background music (soft)
8. Export in multiple formats

### Distribution:
1. Upload to YouTube
2. Create playlist
3. Add to documentation
4. Share with staff
5. Include in training materials
