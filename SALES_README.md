# 🛒 JOECASHIER: The Sovereign POS System
### Premium. Private. Powerful.

**JOECASHIER** is a next-generation Point of Sale and Inventory Management system designed for businesses that value speed, design, and data ownership. Unlike traditional cloud-based POS systems that trap you in monthly subscriptions and harvest your data, JOECASHIER puts the power back in your hands.

---

## 🌟 Why JOECASHIER?

### 1. **Own Your Business, Own Your Data**
No cloud dependencies. Your data stays on your machine or your private cloud. No one monitors your sales, your margins, or your customer lists.

### 2. **Luxury Bento-Grid Interface**
Aesthetics shouldn't be an afterthought. JOECASHIER features a stunning, glassmorphic UI that isn't just beautiful—it's optimized for high-intensity retail environments.

### 3. **Zero Latency**
Built with React 18 and highly optimized SQLite/Postgres backends. Search 10,000+ products in under 5ms. 

### 4. **One-Time Investment**
Stop paying "rent" for your software. JOECASHIER is a sustainable asset for your business, not a monthly liability.

## 🚀 Key Features

### ⚡ **High-Speed Checkout**
- **Smarter Search**: Debounced quick-find (F2) finds products as fast as you can type.
- **Barcode Ready**: Seamless integration with physical scanners.
- **Dual Pricing**: Instant toggle between Retail and Wholesale pricing.
- **Cart Management**: Quick quantity adjustments and removal.

### 📦 **Inventory Intelligence**
- **Dynamic Alerts**: Automatic "Low Stock" warnings to prevent lost sales.
- **Bulk Management**: Professional grid for managing thousands of SKUs.
- **Category Hierarchy**: Organize your store your way.

### 👥 **Customer Loyalty**
- **Debt Tracking**: Manage customer accounts and balances.
- **Purchase History**: Know what your customers want before they ask.
- **Quick Profiles**: Add customers on the fly during checkout.

### 📊 **Deep Analytics**
- **Visual Insights**: Stunning Recharts-powered dashboard showing revenue, profit, and trends.
- **Audit Logs**: Track every action for total revenue integrity.
- **Export Power**: One-click export to Excel and PDF.

### 🖨️ **Professional Printing**
- **Thermal Receipts**: Optimized for 80mm and 58mm printers.
- **A4 Invoices**: High-quality document generation for wholesale clients.
- **Custom Designer**: Drag-and-drop invoice elements.

- **Engine**: TypeScript 5.x & React 18

- **Database**: SQLite (Local) / Supabase (Cloud)
- **Icons**: Lucide-React
- **UI Components**: Radix UI
